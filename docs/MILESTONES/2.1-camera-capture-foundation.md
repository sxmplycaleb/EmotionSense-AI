# Milestone Report: 2.1 — Camera/Capture Foundation

## Objective

Provide a front-facing, local-only camera preview foundation for later face-detection work without implementing detection, inference, capture persistence, or transmission.

## Implemented

- Riverpod-injected `CameraService` abstraction and a Flutter `camera` adapter.
- Explicit permission, initialization, ready, unavailable, error, paused, and stopped states.
- One front-camera controller at a time, with duplicate-start protection and deterministic release on stop, lifecycle interruption, route disposal, and provider disposal.
- A minimal camera screen with clear permission, recovery, error, pause, and live-preview states.
- Android camera permission and an iOS camera usage description.

## Privacy Boundary

The adapter enables preview only. It has no photo/video capture, image stream, filesystem, database, cache, analytics, network, or logging API. It creates no application-owned camera artifact. Camera frames remain within the camera plugin's active preview resources and are released with the controller. This preserves ADR-0009, ADR-0010, and ADR-0012.

## Known Limitations

- No physical device or emulator camera was available for manual preview, orientation, or permission-flow verification.
- iOS compilation and device verification were not run.
- Camera permission settings deep-linking is intentionally not added; restricted-state guidance directs the user to device settings, avoiding an extra dependency.

## Scope Review

No face detection, landmarks, MediaPipe, TensorFlow Lite, model artifact, inference, emotion classification, confidence score, analytics, persistence, backend, or authentication feature was implemented.

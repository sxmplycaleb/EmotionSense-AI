# Dataset Validation References

**Milestone:** 1.1 Dataset Validation  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-04  
**Citation style:** IEEE-style numbered references

This bibliography consolidates the sources used by [DATASET_COMPARISON.md](DATASET_COMPARISON.md), [DATASET_RECOMMENDATION.md](DATASET_RECOMMENDATION.md), and [DATASET_GOVERNANCE.md](DATASET_GOVERNANCE.md). Dataset statistics and access terms are taken from original papers, official project pages, or current license agreements wherever possible. A repository mirror or dataset catalogue is not treated as authority for image rights.

## Evidence classification

### Facts

- Dataset composition and annotation claims are sourced to original peer-reviewed papers and official project documentation.
- Access and usage claims are sourced to current official pages or agreements where available.
- Scientific publication and legal permission are distinct: a paper can document a corpus without granting image or commercial-use rights.

### Assumptions

- Links and agreements reflect the authoritative public material available on the review date; an executed agreement may add stricter terms.
- DOI landing pages and institutional project pages are preferred as durable references, but their future availability is not guaranteed.

### Trade-offs

- Primary sources maximize authority but sometimes omit operational details found in later surveys or repository documentation.
- A consolidated bibliography improves consistency, while dataset-specific access records and signed agreements must still be maintained in the governance register.

### Open questions

- Which versioned license or signed agreement will govern each dataset actually obtained by the project?
- Should permanent archival identifiers be added for official pages that lack DOI-backed records, where archiving is permitted?

## Source-quality and legal interpretation note

Dataset papers establish scientific properties, but a paper’s statement that data are “available” is not a license grant. Current official terms control access and permitted use. Where the official terms could not be obtained or were ambiguous, these documents label commercial use and redistribution as **unresolved**, not permitted. This is a conservative project decision, not legal advice.

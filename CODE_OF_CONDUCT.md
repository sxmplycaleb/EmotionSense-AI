# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the EmotionSense AI community welcoming, safe, and equitable. We value the dignity, rights, experience, and contributions of every participant, regardless of identity, background, ability, education, experience level, or other status.

Everyone participating in good faith and following this Code of Conduct receives the same opportunity to contribute.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Engage kindly, honestly, and with respect for different perspectives.
- Give and receive constructive feedback with care.
- Take responsibility for their words, actions, and contributions.
- Respect consent, privacy, confidentiality, and personal boundaries.
- Credit sources and collaborators appropriately.
- Correct mistakes and participate in repairing harm when possible.
- Protect user dignity and avoid overstating what emotion-estimation systems can determine.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or unwanted personal attention.
- Insults, demeaning comments, personal attacks, or discriminatory stereotyping.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or conduct inappropriate to the community.
- Sharing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging violence, self-harm, or other endangerment.
- Impersonation or evasion of an enforcement action.
- Plagiarism or deliberate failure to credit contributed work.
- Spam, misleading promotion, or communication that irresponsibly amplifies harmful material.
- Any conduct that reasonably threatens the safety or well-being of the community.

## Reporting an Issue

Possible violations should be reported privately to a Community Moderator. Until a dedicated conduct-reporting address is published, contact the repository owner through a private method listed on their GitHub profile and request a confidential reporting channel. Do not include incident details in a public issue or discussion.

Reports involving the repository owner should be directed to another appointed Community Moderator. If no alternate moderator is yet listed, preserve the evidence privately until an independent contact is published rather than disclosing it publicly.

Moderators will protect confidentiality and safety, share information only as necessary to investigate, and avoid conflicts of interest. They will seek a timely, fair response while recognizing that not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation.

## Enforcement

Depending on context, impact, and prior behavior, moderators may apply one or more of these measures:

1. **Private warning:** Clarify the violation, expected behavior, and appropriate repair.
2. **Temporary limits:** Restrict specific interactions or community activities for a defined period.
3. **Temporary suspension:** Remove access until stated conditions for return are met.
4. **Permanent removal:** Revoke access when severe harm or repeated violations make safe participation untenable.

Moderators may skip steps when needed to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, applied consistently, and communicated only as broadly as safety and confidentiality permit.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents EmotionSense AI in public or private spaces, including repository activity, reviews, discussions, events, and official communications.
